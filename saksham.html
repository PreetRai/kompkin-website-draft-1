Making every classroom visible.

A project to digitise all classrooms of Pune district schools serving students with hearing impairment.

25government-recognised schools
2,300students supported
111eLearning sets handed over

A digital district for students with hearing impairment.

Rotary Club of Pune Katraj began the project after an experimental installation showed what animated visual content could do for students and teachers.

The syllabus was developed specifically for these learners. Teachers were trained through a three-day activity-based programme of lectures, presentations and demonstrations.

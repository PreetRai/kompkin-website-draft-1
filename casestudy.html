Case study / Tata Technologies

301 schools. 166,000 students. One focused deadline.

Tata Technologies, Rotary Club of Poona North and Kompkin worked together to bring eLearning to remote village schools.

301remote schools
166Kstudents reached
44K kmtravelled by engineers

From assessment to installation in four weeks.

After school visits, review meetings and a detailed assessment, the partners finalised the scope, school selection norms, installation routes and responsibilities.

Teams travelled difficult terrain, crossed rivers and worked early mornings and late nights to complete the project before the deadline. Teachers and students were trained at every school.

  • 408 installations completed across 301 schools
  • 3,000+ installation documents submitted
  • Sponsors recognised through school plaques and software visibility

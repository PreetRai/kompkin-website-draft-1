About Kompkin

Education should meet students where they are.

We build practical learning tools that help schools make quality education more accessible, engaging and useful.

Our reason for being

Technology with a social motive.

Formed with a vision to take technology-led quality education to the masses, SumConcepts is the organisation behind Kompkin.

Our team brings together educators, psychologists, teachers and career counsellors to create learning experiences that work in the classroom and beyond it.
